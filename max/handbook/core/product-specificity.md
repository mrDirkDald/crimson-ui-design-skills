# Product Specificity

A distinctive UI is not necessarily a visually loud UI.

## Product fingerprints

Find what makes the product materially different:
- entity model;
- primary workflow;
- unusual data/state;
- repeated user ritual;
- domain vocabulary;
- environment;
- constraints;
- media/content type.

Translate those into structure, controls, states, typography, or interaction.

## Genericity test

Remove branding and ask whether the layout could be a VPN, finance dashboard, game launcher, and AI SaaS simultaneously. If yes, strengthen product-grounded decisions.
