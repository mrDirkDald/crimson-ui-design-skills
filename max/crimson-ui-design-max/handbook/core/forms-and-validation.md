# Forms and Validation

## Labels

Placeholders are not durable labels. Use persistent labels when users must identify fields after input.

## Validation timing

Validate on submit, blur, or live depending on the cost and predictability of feedback. Do not show aggressive errors while the user has not had a chance to finish typing.

## Error recovery

Keep valid inputs. Focus or summarize errors when necessary. Explain what to do next.

## Submit

Prevent duplicate requests. Show processing state. Distinguish success, partial success, and failure.

## Sensitive actions

Use confirmation when consequences are high and not easily reversible. Prefer undo for safe reversible actions.
