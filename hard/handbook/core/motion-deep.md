# Motion Deep Guide

Motion should preserve causality. Users should see what changed, where it came from, and where it went.

## Microinteraction
Press, toggle, selection, expansion, and loading feedback should feel immediate. Keep direct manipulation responsive.

## Spatial transitions
Use motion to explain hierarchy or continuity when panels, sheets, cards, or routes transform. Do not animate every layout property if opacity/transform can communicate the same relationship more smoothly.

## Expressive motion
Brand motion can be stronger on marketing/cultural experiences. Define one dominant motion thesis and keep utility interactions quieter.

## Scroll
Scroll-driven animation must not steal scroll control or make reading physically tiring. Pinned sections need a content/narrative reason and a usable reduced-motion alternative.
